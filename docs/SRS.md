## **LNMIIT Heritage Application Explore the Legacy** 

The LNMIIT Heritage Application can become a digital heritage and exploration platform for The LNM Institute of Information Technology that combines: 

- Institutional history 

- Campus exploration 

- Academic ecosystem 

- Student life 

- Innovation & achievements 

- Digital heritage preservation 

- Future-ready audio-guided smart tours 

The portal should not feel like a normal institute website. It should feel like a **living digital campus museum + intelligent campus guide** . 

## **1. Vision of the Portal Primary Objectives** 

The portal should: 

1. Introduce LNMIIT to prospective students, parents, visitors, alumni, and researchers 

2. Showcase the institute’s heritage, culture, infrastructure, and achievements 

3. Offer immersive digital campus exploration 

4. Preserve institutional memory 

5. Provide a future foundation for AI/audio-guided heritage navigation
