## Media provenance

Files are downloaded from the same official conference host, with no watermark removal or visual alteration:

- `https://icmc-2026.lnmiit.ac.in/centralplaza.jpg`: central plaza; visible Broken Aperture watermark.
- `https://icmc-2026.lnmiit.ac.in/lnmiit2.jpg`: aerial campus photograph.
- `https://icmc-2026.lnmiit.ac.in/hostel1.png`: hostel room.
- `https://icmc-2026.lnmiit.ac.in/hostel2.png`: hostel exterior.

The original low-resolution residential images are displayed as supplied by that source. Dates and other photographer credits are not specified.

Official photographs under `public/media/official/` were downloaded from `lnmiit.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/`, resized only where wider than 1920 px and converted to WebP. `film-*.webp` are unretouched 1920x1080 frames of the official campus film, `original-media/lnmiit_view.mp4`, also published at `https://lnmiit.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/lnmiit_view.mp4`. `rubaroo.webp` has its white poster margins trimmed. Stock images on the official site were deliberately not used.

`public/media/campus-reference.webp` is the AI-generated illustration kept unedited at `original-media/campus-approved.png`, with its legend cropped and its three baked labels painted out so that the corrected names can be shown as pins.

## Motto

The homepage shows "Excellence our motto, Discipline our way." from `motto` in `src/data/content.js`. The wording comes from Wikipedia, which cites no source. It did not appear on any of the 222 pages in LNMIIT's sitemap or in 38 institutional PDFs checked on 2 November 2026 (Vision 2035, AQARs, manuals, newsletters, NIRF reports). Confirm it with the institute.

## Interpretation choices

- Eight department entries reflect the directory plus the AI & DS official department page; this is not an enrolment or live statistic.
- CCE was offered as a programme before its departmental establishment in 2022. The timeline labels the departmental event precisely.
- UG overview prose and programme tables on the official site differ: the detailed current programme listing and AI & DS programmes page explicitly list AI and AI & DS; these are included with official links, without guessing seat counts or approval footnotes.
- No unsupported construction dates, former-director lists, invented student testimonials or hypothetical SRS timeline events are used.
- The heritage text is a short synthesis. Full official wording remains at the linked sources.
